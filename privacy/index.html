Personal Ops Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 22, 2026

Personal Ops is a personal productivity and reminder service that uses SMS messaging to provide task confirmations, list responses, and reminders requested by authorized users.

Information Collected

Personal Ops may collect and store information necessary to provide the service, including:

  • Mobile phone numbers
  • SMS message content sent to and from Personal Ops
  • Tasks, reminders, and list information submitted by the user
  • Message delivery and interaction records

This information is used only to operate, maintain, and improve Personal Ops and to provide the features requested by the user.

Mobile Information and Messaging Consent

Mobile phone numbers, SMS opt-in information, and messaging consent will not be shared, sold, rented, or provided to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Service providers may process information only as necessary to provide technical services such as SMS delivery and infrastructure hosting.

SMS Messaging

Personal Ops sends transactional and user-requested SMS messages, including command responses, task confirmations, and scheduled reminders.

Message frequency varies based on user activity and the reminders requested by the user.

Message and data rates may apply.

Users may reply STOP to opt out of SMS messages and HELP for assistance.

Data Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect stored information and access to Personal Ops.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Personal Ops evolves. The current version will be published at this page.
